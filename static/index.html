Sensitive data we store

        
  • Phone numbers and emails of passengers
  • Phone numbers and license numbers of drivers
  • Payment history

        
Drivers see a passenger's phone only during the ride.

      
Transaction example

        
Completing a ride runs two statements together:

        
  1. UPDATE rides SET status='completed'
  2. INSERT INTO payments …

        
If either fails, both are rolled back, so a ride can't be completed without a payment record.

      
Limitation

        
Passengers and drivers log in with a phone number only. Our schema has no password field.

        
To fix: SMS codes or a password hash column.
